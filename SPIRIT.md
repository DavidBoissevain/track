# The spirit of Pointly Track

This file says what the app is for and what it should never turn into. If a
feature idea does not fit on this page, it probably does not belong in the app.

## The problem

As a consultant you agree an amount of time per project. Four days a week on
one, four days a month on another. The hard part is not writing the hours down,
it is noticing partway through the week that one project has had almost nothing
and another has quietly eaten the whole week.

This used to be a spreadsheet. Hours per day per project went in, and a bar
chart came out that showed where every project stood.

## The goal

**Spend enough time on each project, and not too much.**

Not more billable hours. Not detailed reports for a manager. Just a clear
picture, in one look, of where you are against what you planned.

## How it works

1. You set a capacity per project, for example two days a week, or four days a
   month. You pick the period per project, because some projects make sense per
   week and others per month.
2. At the end of the day you type the hours you did per project.
3. Every project gets a bar:
   - **How tall the bar is** shows the capacity you gave that project. A four
     day a week project is twice as tall as a two day a week one, so you see
     the big commitments at a glance.
   - **Grey** is what is still left to do.
   - **Blue** fills up from the bottom as you log hours.
   - **Red** sticks out above the top of the bar once you go over capacity.

Nothing untouched turns red. A project you have not worked on yet is simply all
grey, which is information too.

## Rules we hold ourselves to

- **Free for everyone.** No paid plan, no trial that runs out, no features
  behind a wall.
- **No tracking.** No ad trackers and no analytics that follow people around.
  Hours are stored so you can get them back, and for no other reason.
- **No timers.** You type the hours you did. Nothing runs in the background.
- **Few features on purpose.** Every extra option is one more thing to
  understand. If a feature does not help you answer "am I on track this week",
  the answer is no.
- **Yours to leave with.** Your data is yours. Delete the account and it all
  goes with it.

## Part of the Pointly suite

Pointly is a small family of tools that all follow the same idea: free, to the
point, and easy to understand on the first try. SprintVotes does planning
poker, SprintRetro does retrospectives, and more will follow. They share a look
so they feel like one set of tools.

## What this is not

- Not a billing or invoicing tool.
- Not a team surveillance tool. It is for the person doing the work.
- Not a project management tool. No tasks, no boards, no dependencies.
- Not a timesheet you submit to anyone.
